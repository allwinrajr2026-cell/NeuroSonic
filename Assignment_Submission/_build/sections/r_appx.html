Appendix D: Physical Validation Protocol (Summary)

None of these tests has been performed yet. Each scenario is repeated five times; every trial is recorded, including failures and missed alerts; latency is measured, not estimated. Safety rules: the tester walks with a sighted companion or their usual mobility aid; vehicles are observed only from a safe place; NeuroSonic is never relied on.
