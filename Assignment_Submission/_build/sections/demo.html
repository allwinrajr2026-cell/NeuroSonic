Demonstration script (about 10 minutes)

  1. Base paper (2 min). Run the re-implemented WOAD detector on a recorded NIR + depth sequence (Settings → Research tools → WOAD replay); show the equivalence test result and the evaluation table.
  2. Launch (1 min). START_NEUROSONIC.ps1; show the printed detector (RF-DETR, cuda, fp16) and the iPhone address.
  3. Walking-path awareness (3 min). On the phone (or the laptop browser with the demo clip), press Start; show "appears clear", "Caution", "Stop", and the event "A person is moving into your path from the left"; pan the camera over a static scene to show that no event is raised.
  4. Voice and navigation (2 min). Say "navigate to CMC Hospital"; confirm; show the offline route and spoken turn instruction; show that it works with the internet disconnected.
  5. Honesty and limits (2 min). Show the "How it works" screen; state what is not yet verified.
