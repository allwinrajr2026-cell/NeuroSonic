The Base Paper: WOAD

System overview

WOAD addresses a stated tension: wearable obstacle-avoidance devices find it hard to be reliable, responsive, long-lasting and usable at the same time [[woad]]. Its answer has four parts.

  • Sensing glasses. Self-developed glasses of about 400 g, including an 80 g battery, carry a Synexens CS30 RGB-ToF module. Its near-infrared (NIR) camera and ToF depth sensor share a 100°×75° field of view at 640×480 pixels; depth is measured from 0.1 to 5 m.
  • Depth-aided compression. To reduce transmission delay to the phone, a reinforcement-learning agent (discrete soft actor-critic [[dsac]]) uses obstacle status from consecutive depth frames to decide whether to send a frame and at what quality. The authors report a compression ratio above 95 %.
  • FPGA. The compression policy runs on a custom 28 nm board with a multi-float-point vector streaming architecture; the authors report a latency of 0.095 ms and over 50 % lower power than their reference.
  • Cross-modal detector on the phone. A Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra runs a TensorFlow Lite version of a YOLOv5-based detector [[yolov5]] with two input streams (NIR and colour-mapped depth) fused by transformer blocks [[transformer]]. Alerts are spoken at three hazard levels (attention, warning, danger), and three vibration motors on the forehead and temples indicate direction; the vibration frequency encodes distance (200 Hz below 3 m, 150 Hz above).

Detector architecture

{{f-arch}} shows the detector as we verified it on the released checkpoint (run exp72). The NIR image passes through the first three YOLOv5s layers and the depth image through a separate stem of the same structure, each producing a 64×160×160 feature map. Both are scaled by a fixed 0.5 gate, average-pooled to 8×8, and flattened into 128 tokens of 64 dimensions with a learned positional embedding. Eight transformer blocks with eight attention heads let every NIR token attend to every depth token. The output tokens are reshaped, upsampled, and used to re-weight each stream's features; the streams are concatenated, fused by a convolution, and passed through the standard YOLOv5s backbone, PAN neck and detection head, which outputs 25,200 candidate boxes for three classes (person, Ebike, Car). The checkpoint has 7,521,992 parameters (the paper reports 7,497,914 for its model [[woad]]; the small difference may come from a different training run or from counting, and does not affect the analysis).
