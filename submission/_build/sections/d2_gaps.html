Cons of the Base Work and Gap Identification

The strengths of WOAD are real: an integrated hardware–software system, a principled two-modality detector, energy-aware compression, and an unusually thorough user study [[woad]]. The re-implementation also exposed weaknesses. We separate cons (properties of the base work) from gaps (what is missing for the target use: an ordinary phone, Indian streets, low cost, offline operation) and map each gap to a contribution.

Cons observed

  1. Hardware lock-in. The detector requires NIR images from the CS30 sensor and depth colour-mapped by the vendor SDK's own look-up table (not a standard colour map, and with undocumented range settings). An ordinary RGB camera cannot drive it: feeding RGB as "NIR" with a fake depth image is out of distribution, and our ablation shows that NIR carries most of the signal.
  2. Reproducibility. The reported detector accuracy cannot be re-measured with the published data, the scripts did not run with current libraries, and the run's results files are missing.
  3. Generalisation. On the published scenes, person AP@0.5 is 0.17–0.22; cyclists and distant people are mostly missed. For a warning system, low recall on people is a safety-relevant weakness.
  4. Exposure sensitivity. Darker sensor recordings remove most detections.
  5. Narrow class set. Three classes (person, Ebike, Car) in the released model.
  6. No distance in the released detector code. The paper's device encodes distance in vibration, but the released detector outputs only class, box and confidence; distance would need the raw 16-bit depth, which the colourised image does not preserve.
  7. Frame-by-frame semantics. The released detector code has no tracking, so it cannot distinguish an object moving into the path from the apparent motion caused by the wearer's own walking.
  8. Obstacle-only. There is no route guidance or speech-command interface in the released code.

Gaps G1 and G2 are decisive: they mean that the base model cannot be the live detector of a phone-based system. The proposed work therefore keeps WOAD as a verified research baseline and builds a new perception path for the phone camera, while addressing G3–G8 in the system design.
